Business Verification & Contract

Contract Process
Select your preferred membership package
Alibaba prepares the contract
Review contract details (benefits, fees, package)
Complete payment via bank transfer
Step 1.1
Submit Business Information
Provide your business registration certificate along with owner details to verify your company identity.
Step 1.2
Choose Your Membership Package
Work with your Account Manager to select the membership plan that best fits your business goals and budget. Alibaba will prepare the contract based on your chosen package.
Step 1.3
Review & Sign the Contract
Carefully review all contract terms — benefits, costs, and package details. Once confirmed, complete payment via bank transfer to Alibaba.com using the contract reference number.
2

Storefront Design & Build

30 business days
Step 2.1
Prepare Design Materials
Provide your company profile, product catalog, product images, and factory/workshop photos. Share your preferred design direction and visual concept for the minisite.
⏱ ~10 days
Step 2.2
Draft Minisite Design
Alibaba's design team creates the first draft of your storefront. You review the draft and provide feedback — revisions are made until you're satisfied before moving forward.
⏱ ~7 days
Step 2.3
Revise & Refine (If Needed)
Based on your feedback, the design team makes adjustments. Product keywords are researched and built. Technical attribute forms are created for each product category. The full catalog, product list, and images are compiled.
⏱ ~7 days
Step 2.4
Category & Product Page Design
Product categories are structured and individual product pages are designed with optimized layouts, images, and keyword-rich content ready for upload.
⏱ ~7 days
3

Product Upload & Official Launch

5 business days
Step 3.1
Upload First 100 Products
You provide product details and select target keywords. Alibaba's team uploads your first 100 products with optimized titles, descriptions, and attributes for maximum search visibility.
⏱ ~3 days
Step 3.2
Store Verification & Activation
Alibaba verifies and activates your storefront. The contract timeline officially begins only after store activation — you are not charged during the setup period.
⏱ ~2 days
Step 3.3
Handover & Go Live
Your fully built storefront is handed over to you for final review. Once approved, your store goes live on Alibaba.com and your membership contract officially begins.
